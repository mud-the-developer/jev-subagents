# Primary references and what they actually support

These references motivate the audit and the requirements in [METHOD.md](METHOD.md). They do **not** retroactively turn a small SQL role-workflow pilot into an official model-routing, semantic-SQL or coding leaderboard result. Read/fetched during this audit; original data and scores are unchanged.

Section5: random routing comparator, separate validation/training and held-out benchmarks. | This paper studies a strong/weak model pair and threshold-based routing. ## Reproducibility scope

`analyze.py` names R1/R2/R7/R8/R9 in its output. It reads the immutable published metadata from commit6a35c27a38594ada1b43506eeb3b35b2e88a9cc6 and records its SHA256. It makes0provider calls and executes0SQLqueries. Statistical formulas and bank enumeration have assert-based self-checks. [RESULTS.md](RESULTS.md) separates actual historical measurements from hypothetical offline-bank diagnostics and unexecuted future coding gates.

The original BIRD data are attributed to the BIRD-SQL team under CC-BY-SA4.0. No raw questions, evidence, schema, DBs, gold/model SQL, phase reports, private profile bodies, credentials or personal paths are copied into this audit. Numeric IDs/hashes/counts and public method references are provenance. Source/report generation does not authorize another model trial, package/runtime install, cloud evaluation, security relaxation, main merge or release.
